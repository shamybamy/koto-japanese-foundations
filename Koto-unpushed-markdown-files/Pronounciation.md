#japanese

Consonants

Same as eng:
- b = about same as eng
	- button (botan)
- z = about same as eng
	- zoom (zumu)
- g = about same as eng
	- gown (gaun)
- h  = about same as eng
	- harp (haapu)
- m = about same as eng
	- marathon
- n = about same as eng
	- knife (naifu)
- s = about same as eng
	- slipper (suripa)
- j = y sound from eng
	- yard (yaado)
- nng = n pronounced with back of tongue
	- ring (ri n gu)

Slightly diff from eng:
- d = pronounced with tongue touching upper teeth (a bit of t sound, less percussive)
	- day (like a harder T, softer D)
- t = same as d, tongue touching upper teeth (less percussive, less air coming out of mouth)
- p = less air, shorter/more lightweight
- k = same as p

Slightly diff from eng + palatisation
palatisation = tongue moves up towards hard palate, kinda making hissing sound (like k in key vs k in cut / y sound in you)
- gyu  = kinda making like a slight giii sound before the vowel
	- gallery (gyalary)
- kya = kinda making a slight kii sound before vowel
	- cuba (kyuba)
- byu = kinda making slight bii sound before vowel
	- buffet (byufe)
- myu = kinda making slight mii sound before vowel
	- musical (myugikaru)
- nyu = same shit with nii
	- new york (nyu yorku)
- pyu = same with pii
	- puritan (pyuritan)

Weird one
- ? = shutting/popping open vocal cord for emphasis
	- atsu vs atsu?

New, not rlly in eng
- sh = basically palatised version of sh from english
	- same sh sound but keep tongue high
	- shiteru
- ch = basically palatised ch sound
	- same ch but keep tongue high
	- chicken (tchiken)
- zh = basically palatised zh/j sound
	- same but keep tongue high
	- jazz (jazu)
- h = palatised hh sound (like hissing)
	- like squeeze mid tongue to palate
	- heater (heeta)
- f = no teeth f sound HAHA, between f and h, makes it a bit shorter (eng brings lower lip to upper teeth)
	- bring lips close together and slightly pull in corner of mouth
	- flamingo (fuulamingo)
- u = same mouth position as f, and say uuu. (dont stick out lips like eng)
	- utsu
- w = same mouth position as u but say wa
	- wire (waiya) / why (wai)

Very diff from eng
- nng = diff from the other nng. basically makes the preceding vowel kinda nasally just before u make the n sound and you tongue touches the soft palate
	- penguin (panginng)
	- eng king vs jp kiinn (gold)
- r = a bit like L sound
	- basically bring tip of tongue slightly behind where it is when u say d
	- rocket (lroketto)



Vowels
a – i – u – e – o
- a = in between fawn and fan
	- just say pan (bread)
- i = same as eng
- u = same mouth position as f, don’t let lips pout, curl in corners of mouth
	- sue vs su
- e = basically same as eng, but tongue kinda between ee in sit and ehh in set
	- elevator (elebata), air con (ea kon)
- o = same as eng but only first part. (go we say go-u. just keep the first part)

** if two vowels or consonants in a row, double the length of pronounciation (pause)
	- tori (bird) vs toori (street) and saka (slope) vs sak-ka (author)
	- sometimes we whisper the vowel – christmas eve = k(u)rismasevu
