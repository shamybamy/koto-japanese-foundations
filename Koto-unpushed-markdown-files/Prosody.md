#japanese
Basically information other than pronunciation that changes meaning:

English:
- Pitch/melody
- Loudness/stress
- Length/rhythm
1.  Al three are usually in conjunction for:
	1. Stress/emphasis
		- I REALLY like ice cream
	2. Or to change word meaning (or just is the way to pronounce)
		- conTRACT (get a disease) CONtract(agreement)
		- uniVERsity (just how its pronounced)
		- reSEARCH vs REsearch (just diff accent)

Japanese:
1. Stress/emphasis
	- Only done through pitch/melody
		- Usually part we wanna emphasise is higher
2. Changing word meaning
	- Still use word length/rhythm, not rlly loudness
		- aME - candy (down up) vs Ame - rain (up down)
		- Also the double consonants and double vowels means double length
			- to-o-kyo not to-kyo
